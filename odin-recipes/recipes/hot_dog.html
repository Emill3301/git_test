HOT DOG

Description

A hot dog is a popular fast food dish consisting of a grilled, steamed, or boiled sausage (such as a frankfurter or wiener) placed inside a partially sliced oblong bun. It is typically topped with condiments like ketchup, mustard, and relish, and enjoyed worldwide at sports events and barbecues.

  • Frankfurters
  • Yellow mustard
  • Tomato ketchup
