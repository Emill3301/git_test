BURGER

Description

This towering gourmet burger features a succulent, flame-grilled Angus beef patty cooked to juicy perfection. It is nestled inside a pillowy, toasted brioche bun subtly glistening with melted artisanal butter. Layers of crisp, hand-torn iceberg lettuce and vine-ripened tomato slices provide a refreshing crunch. Tangy dill pickle ribbons and thinly shaved red onions add a sharp contrast to the rich meat. A blanket of mature cheddar cheese melts seamlessly over the patty, creating a gooey cascade. The masterpiece is generously slathered with a signature smoky house sauce that ties every ingredient together. Each bite delivers a perfect harmony of savory, sweet, smoky, and texturally diverse flavorse
